# Reference verification for the English manuscript

Checked on 2026-09-11 against DOI-resolved publisher records. The claims retained in Section 1.3 are limited to what the publisher title, abstract, and bibliographic metadata support.

| Ref. | Verified metadata | Primary record | Claim supported in the manuscript |
|---|---|---|---|
| [1] | R. H. Lasseter, “MicroGrids,” *2002 IEEE Power Engineering Society Winter Meeting*, pp. 305–308, 2002. DOI `10.1109/PESW.2002.985003`. | [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/985003/) | The paper presents the MicroGrid as a cluster of loads and microsources operating as one controllable system and interacting with the utility as a controlled power-system cell. |
| [2] | J. M. Guerrero, J. C. Vasquez, J. Matas, L. G. de Vicuña, and M. Castilla, “Hierarchical Control of Droop-Controlled AC and DC Microgrids—A General Approach Toward Standardization,” *IEEE Transactions on Industrial Electronics*, 58(1), 158–172, 2011. DOI `10.1109/TIE.2010.2066534`. | [IEEE Xplore DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIE.2010.2066534) | The title and publisher metadata support the manuscript’s narrow statement that the work discusses hierarchical control for AC and DC microgrids. No broader performance claim is attributed to it. |
| [3] | D. Tenfen and E. C. Finardi, “A mixed integer linear programming model for the energy management problem of microgrids,” *Electric Power Systems Research*, 122, 19–28, 2015. DOI `10.1016/j.epsr.2014.12.019`. | [Elsevier ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378779614004659) | The abstract explicitly describes a MILP energy-management model that minimizes operating cost over a planning horizon subject to economic and technical constraints. |
| [4] | M. B. Sigalo, A. C. Pillai, S. Das, and M. DOI `10.3390/en14196212`. | [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1073/14/19/6212) | The title directly supports the manuscript’s statement that the study applies MILP to battery control in a grid-connected microgrid. |
| [5] | A. Parisio, E. Rikos, and L. Glielmo, “Stochastic model predictive control for economic/environmental operation management of microgrids: An experimental case study,” *Journal of Process Control*, 43, 24–37, 2016. DOI `10.1016/j.jprocont.2016.04.008`. | [Elsevier ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959152416300324) | The title and publisher metadata support the statement that the study combines stochastic model-predictive control with microgrid operational management and reports an experimental case study. |

No citation is used as evidence for the contest’s settlement rule, timestamp convention, storage-efficiency interpretation, future-price availability, or out-of-sample economic performance. Those remain declared working assumptions or unresolved items.
